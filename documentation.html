Architecture

FAQ

Can the Colab tab be closed?

Yes. The website tracker is independent of the Colab tab and continues waiting for the Drive result.

Does progress show exact AI percentage?

No. It represents processing stages because the website does not receive an exact Whisper or Spleeter percentage.
